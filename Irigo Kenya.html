    Tap the card to see the answer. A card you miss goes back to box 1. A card you get right moves up one box. Four boxes means the card is learned.

    Why you review on a schedule

    Modelled forgetting curve

    This is a simple model, not a measurement of you. It assumes memory fades exponentially and that each review makes it fade about 2.4 times more slowly. The pattern is what matters: reviews spaced further apart work if each one comes before the memory is gone.
