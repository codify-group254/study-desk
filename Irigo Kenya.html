Innovating Through Economic Challenges

Smart Water Solutions Helping Kenyan Farmers Do More With Less

IriGoKenya helps people grow crops and plants when water is hard to predict and costs are high. We sells smart watering systems, automatic controllers, and fertilizers that help farmers use less water and spend money wisely. The company also gives advice and tests soil and water. This helps farmers, landscapers, golf courses, and shops keep plants healthy and keep making money, even when times are difficult.

What We Do 🌾 💧

  • Design and install smart irrigation systems that water crops only when and where needed.
  • Provide soil moisture sensors and automated controllers that save water and reduce waste.
  • Make fertilizers in different forms, like tiny beads or liquids that you can pour.
  • Help gardeners, farmers, and the workers who take care of big golf courses and soccer fields.
  • Test the soil and water quality to give the best advice on keeping crops and grass healthy.
  • Support farmers with remote monitoring tools that track irrigation performance in real time.

"When every shilling and every drop counts, smart irrigation is how Kenya keeps growing."

Steps to Achieve the Goal

  1. Identify farms and fields that could benefit from smart irrigation upgrades.
  2. Assess soil type, water sources, and existing irrigation infrastructure.
  3. Design a tailored irrigation plan using sensors, controllers, and efficient piping.
  4. Source affordable, durable irrigation equipment and materials.
  5. Train farmers and field workers on installing and operating the system.
  6. Install sensors and automated controllers across the target fields.
  7. Test the system to ensure accurate moisture readings and consistent water flow.
  8. Monitor performance and adjust watering schedules based on real-time data.
  9. Gather feedback from farmers to refine and improve the system.
  10. Expand the project to more farms and communities in need of water efficiency.
  11. Seek partnerships or sponsorships to sustain and scale the initiative.
  12. Document and share success stories to inspire wider adoption of smart irrigation.
